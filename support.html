Bubble Fun Support

Bubble Fun is an offline bubble-popping game for iPhone and iPad.

How do I play?

Tap a bubble to pop it. Touch and hold empty space to grow a rainbow bubble, then release. Pop quickly to build combos and fill the Super Pop ring.

Why is there no sound?

Check the Sound Effects toggle inside the game’s Settings screen, your device volume, and the Ring/Silent setting.

How do I reset my scores?

Open Settings, scroll to Progress, and choose Reset Scores.

Does the game need internet?

No. Bubble Fun works entirely offline.
