Bubble Fun Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Bubble Fun does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game has no advertising, analytics, user accounts, tracking technologies, or third-party data services.

Information stored on your device

Bubble Fun stores game settings, high scores, total bubbles popped, games played, and longest combo locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game features and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Children’s privacy

Because Bubble Fun does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Network access

The game works offline and does not require an internet connection.

Your choices

You can reset locally stored scores from the Settings screen. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored game data.

Changes

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.
